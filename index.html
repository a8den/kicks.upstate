FAQ

Are your shoes authentic?

Yes. Every pair is checked before it gets listed. Ask for extra pictures of anything you want to see, like tags, stitching, insoles or the box label.

Do you ship?

Yes, all pairs ship with tracking. Shipping cost depends on where you are, so ask on Snapchat and I'll quote it with your pair.

Can we meet up locally?

Yes. If you're in Upstate New York we can set up a meetup in a public spot. Send me your area on Snapchat.

Why can't I check out on the site?

I handle every sale personally so I can confirm the pair, answer questions, and get it to you the fastest way. It takes a minute on Snapchat.

Can I hold a pair?

Message me about it. Pairs are first come, first served, but we can usually work something out.

Do you do trades?

Send me what you have and what you want from the shelf, and I'll let you know.

How does wholesale work?

Add pairs on the wholesale list, put the sizes you need, and send the request. I'll quote you a per-pair price based on your order, then we set up payment and shipping or pickup.
